Rules

RULES
OF THE SAILING ROUTE RECORD
GDYNIA-ŚWINOUJŚCIE or ŚWINOUJŚCIE-GDYNIA

English translation for information. The binding version is the Polish text of the rules.

1. General provisions

The Sailing Route Record Gdynia-Świnoujście or Świnoujście-Gdynia is managed by:

  • the Route Record Committee,
  • Navy Yacht Club „Kotwica” (JKMW „Kotwica”), Gdynia;

In all matters concerning the Sailing Route Record the above institutions are represented by the Route Record Committee (KRT).
The name of the helmsman who sets a record and information about the yacht will be made public. A record is considered newly set if the time to sail the route is at least 1 minute shorter than the previous one.

2. Participation

Sailing sports craft of 6.5 m length or more may be entered for a record attempt, with no restrictions on sail area, class or type. A record attempt is made individually, at a time chosen by the competitor and stated in the notification.

3. Notification

The intention to attempt the route record must be notified to the Route Record Committee by e‑mail or phone.
Attempts may be made from 1 May to 31 October each year.

The notification must state:

  • the helmsman’s first name and surname,
  • the yacht’s name and sail number,
  • the day and hour of the start.

The helmsman is asked to provide, before the start or after arriving at the finish, additional information on the yacht type, owner and crew. The yacht may be subject to a navigation safety inspection by the Route Record Committee.

4. Route

The record is set on the route GDYNIA-ŚWINOUJŚCIE or ŚWINOUJŚCIE-GDYNIA.

The route is approx. 200 NM long.

Start/finish line in Gdynia:

  • southern entrance head of the commercial port, 3 cables in a southerly direction.

Start/finish line in Świnoujście:

  • eastern entrance head – fairway buoy no. „16”.

The yacht must avoid areas closed to navigation and comply with all regulations that apply to it.

5. Calculation of the route time

To verify that the 200 NM route has been sailed and to calculate the time of a yacht attempting the record precisely, the organiser uses a digital tracking system. Every yacht taking part in an attempt must use the OMNI REGATTA RACE RECORDER app in accordance with the organiser’s instructions. Data from the app are the sole basis for officially confirming the result and the record time. Instructions are available in a dedicated section of the website.

The start and finish times must be entered in the yacht’s logbook. The route time is calculated to the nearest minute. The route time may also be measured by the Route Record Committee.

6. Means of propulsion

During the attempt the yacht may be propelled by wind power only. The Route Record Committee may require engines to be sealed.

7. Other sources of energy

On board only the crew’s muscle power may be used to operate all mechanisms and equipment. Various forms of mechanical and hydraulic transmission are not prohibited. Other sources of energy may be used only for lighting, cooking meals and operating electronic navigation equipment.

8. Crew

The helmsman named in the notification must be on board from the moment of the start until the end of the attempt.

9. Reporting the record

The time taken to sail the route must be reported in writing (VOYAGE CARD, extract from the YACHT LOGBOOK) to the Chairman of the Route Record Committee within 48 hours of arriving at the finish.

10. Ratification of the record

The Route Record Committee ratifies the record. The record is recognised on the day of its ratification and confirmed by a CERTIFICATE.

11. Owner’s responsibility, organiser’s responsibility

  • The safety of the yacht and its crew is the inescapable responsibility of the owner, who must ensure that the yacht is fully seaworthy and manned by an experienced crew physically able to face bad weather. The yacht must have a sound hull, spars, rigging, sails and all gear. The owner must ensure that all safety equipment is properly maintained and stowed, and the crew should know where it is and how to use it.
  • Neither the Special Regulations adopted by the Route Record Committee (KRT) nor the inspection of the yacht under these regulations in any way limit or reduce the full responsibility of the owner or the owner’s representative.
  • The decision to start or to continue a route record attempt is the sole responsibility of each yacht’s helmsman.
  • The Route Record Committee (KRT) accepts no liability for any injury or loss of life of participants or others, or for the loss of or damage to any yacht taking part in the Sailing Route Record. Nor is it liable for any loss or damage resulting from undertaking a record attempt.

13. Public Record Register

  1. A public Register of the Świnoujście–Gdynia Record is kept.
  2. The purpose of the Register is to document the history of the Record and successive verified results and to ensure that information about the Record is transparent and reliable.
  3. If an attempt is recognised as a Record, the Register may publish:
    a) the sailor’s first name and surname,
    b) the yacht’s name,
    c) the date the Record was set,
    d) the time taken to sail the route.
  4. The Register is historical and documentary in nature.
  5. Data in the Register are not published for advertising or direct marketing of the person concerned.

14. Information for participants

  1. Before submitting a notification, the applicant is informed that if the attempt is positively verified, the basic data concerning the Record will be published in the public Register.
  2. The applicant acknowledges that the public Register is kept to document the history of the Record and that the entry for a recognised result may be kept for as long as needed to preserve the historical documentation of the Record.
  3. The legal basis for processing data in keeping and publishing the Register is the legitimate interest of the controller referred to in Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  4. The participant acknowledges that the skipper’s (helmsman’s) name and the yacht’s name will be publicly visible in the live tracking system (OMNI REGATTA RACE RECORDER) during the attempt and, if the attempt succeeds, published in the public Record Register. Detailed rules on personal data processing and the full rights of participants (including the right of access, rectification or objection) are set out in the „Privacy Policy” available in a dedicated section of the website.

15. Historical nature of entries

  1. An entry concerning a recognised Record presents the historical result of a verified attempt.
  2. The sailor later ending their sailing activity, a change of their contact details or other circumstances unrelated to whether the Record was correctly set do not result in the historical entry being removed automatically.
  3. If a change or deletion of data is requested, the controller considers the request in accordance with GDPR, taking into account the historical and documentary nature of the Register and the legitimate interest in preserving information about the Record set.
  4. If preserving the historical entry is legally justified, the controller may refuse to delete it to the extent permitted by GDPR.
  5. If the proceedings show that there are grounds to restrict or delete certain data, the controller will take appropriate action to the extent required by law.

16. Correction of data

  1. If data in the Register are incorrect or out of date, the person concerned may ask the controller to rectify them.
  2. If an error is shown, the controller makes the appropriate correction.
  3. Correcting data does not mean deleting the historical fact that the Record was set.

17. Data protection

Contact details and materials provided to verify an attempt are not published in the public Register unless their publication has a separate legal basis.

The controller applies technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk involved in processing the data.
