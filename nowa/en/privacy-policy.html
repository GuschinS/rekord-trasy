PRIVACY POLICY
Świnoujście–Gdynia Record

Effective from: 1 September 2026

The controller runs an information website about the Świnoujście–Gdynia Record and keeps the public Record Register.

2. Nature of the Record Register

The Świnoujście–Gdynia Record Register is a public, historical register of attempts to set, and of set, sailing records on the Świnoujście–Gdynia route.

The purpose of the Register is to document the history of the record, to allow results to be verified and to ensure that information about records set is transparent and reliable.

If an attempt is positively verified by the Committee under the Record Rules, data concerning the record set may be published in the Register.

3. What data are processed

In connection with notifying an attempt and keeping the Register, the following may be processed in particular:

  1. the sailor’s first name and surname,
  2. the yacht’s name,
  3. the date the record was set,
  4. the time taken to sail the route,
  5. contact details provided by the applicant, where necessary to verify the notification,
  6. information and documents provided in connection with verifying the attempt, to the extent necessary to assess whether the attempt complies with the Rules.

Contact details and documents used to verify a notification are not intended to be made public, unless a separate legal basis allows it.

4. Purposes of processing

Personal data are processed in order to:

  1. receive and handle notifications of Record attempts;
  2. verify that an attempt complies with the Rules;
  3. keep documentation of records set;
  4. keep the historical Record Register;
  5. publicly present verified Record results;
  6. protect the reliability and integrity of the Register;
  7. handle enquiries, requests and any claims related to the operation of the Record.

5. Legal basis

The legal basis for processing personal data in keeping and publishing the Register is Art. 6(1)(f) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council („GDPR”), i.e. the legitimate interest of the controller.

The controller’s legitimate interest consists in particular in keeping a reliable and publicly available historical register of Świnoujście–Gdynia Record results, documenting the history of the record and enabling interested persons to verify its results.

Publication of the first name and surname, the yacht’s name, and the date and time of a verified record is limited to what is necessary for this purpose.

6. Balancing test

Before relying on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, the controller assessed its legitimate interest, the necessity of processing and the impact of processing on the rights and freedoms of data subjects.

The controller takes into account in particular that a person notifying a record attempt does so voluntarily and is informed before notifying that, if verified positively, basic information about the record holder may be published in the historical Record Register.

The scope of published data is limited to data directly related to the sporting result: first name, surname, yacht name, date and time of passage.

The controller does not publish in the Register data such as home address, phone number, e‑mail address, PESEL number or other data not directly related to the Record result. We do not use cookies, tracking systems (such as Google Analytics) or interactive forms. A visit to the website is fully anonymous.

  1. Digital monitoring (tracking) is used on the 200 NM route to verify that the route has been sailed and to calculate the yacht’s time precisely.
  2. As part of live tracking, the following are published on a publicly available map: the yacht’s name and the skipper’s first name and surname together with their current geographical position. The legal basis for this processing is the legitimate interest of the Controller.

7. Public nature of the Register

A person notifying an attempt is informed before notifying that a positively verified result may be entered in the public Register.

An entry in the Register is historical and documentary in nature.

An entry is not changed or deleted merely because time has passed since the record was set.

8. Retention period

Data concerning verified records are kept for as long as necessary to keep the historical Record Register.

Owing to the historical and documentary nature of the Register, data concerning records set may be kept indefinitely, provided there is a legitimate need to preserve the entry and there are no legal grounds requiring its deletion.

Data in the notification documentation that are not needed to keep the Register are kept for as long as necessary to complete the verification and to protect against possible claims, in accordance with applicable law.

9. Recipients of data

Public data in the Register are available to Internet users.

Non‑public data may be available to persons involved in verifying the attempt and to providers of technical services such as hosting, e‑mail or website maintenance, to the extent necessary to provide those services.

10. Rights of data subjects

Data subjects have the rights arising from GDPR, in particular the right to:

  • access their data,
  • rectify their data,
  • restrict processing,
  • erasure of data in cases provided for by law,
  • object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR,
  • lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

These rights may be subject to limitations under GDPR.

In particular, a request for erasure does not automatically remove a historical entry from the Register. The controller assesses in each case whether the conditions for erasure under Art. 17 GDPR are met and whether there are overriding legitimate grounds for further processing.

11. Objection

Where data are processed on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, the data subject may object on grounds relating to their particular situation.

The controller considers the objection in accordance with Art. 21 GDPR.

When considering an objection, the controller assesses in particular the nature of the Register, its historical and documentary purpose, the importance of preserving the reliability of the Record’s history and the arguments presented by the person objecting.

12. Right to lodge a complaint

Data subjects have the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office if they consider that the processing of their personal data infringes GDPR.

13. Security

The controller applies appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure.

14. Changes to the privacy policy

The controller may update this Privacy Policy if the way the website operates, the law or the technical solutions used change.

The current version of the Privacy Policy is published on the website.
