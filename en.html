Has Paszke set a record not to be broken?

On 25 October 2009 Roman Paszke’s crew on the maxi catamaran GEMINI 3 sailed the Świnoujście – Gdynia route in 8 hours, 55 minutes and 50 seconds, improving on their own record from August by more than 4 hours. The average speed was 21 knots, the top speed 33.7 knots.

Marian Kula, head of the Sailing Route Record Committee, waited many years for the 10‑hour barrier to fall and believes GEMINI 3 could sail the route in as little as five hours.

Crew of Gemini 3: skipper – Roman Paszke, navigator – Robert Janecki, Mariusz Siedlecki, Igor Szrubkowski, Mariusz Klupiński, Andrzej Bownik, Grzegorz Zalewski, Jacek Zalewski, Kuba Strzyczkowski.
